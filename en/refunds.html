Refund, Cancellation and Dispute Policy

Effective as of November 27, 2026

This English version is a courtesy translation. If it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

This policy explains how to cancel your Acuarius subscription, when you can request a refund and what to do if you see a charge you do not recognize. It is part of the Terms of Service. Acuarius is a brand operated by Johana Liliana Soler Beltran (Colombia); charges are billed by Aixion Labs Solutions LLC (Texas, United States).

1. Free trial

  • New accounts get a 14-day trial of the Pro plan. To start it, you add a card through Stripe; nothing is charged during the trial.
  • If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the monthly Pro plan is charged the next day at the published price and renews every month until you cancel. Before the first charge you can choose your plan and its add-ons.
  • You can cancel at any time during the trial from your account, at no cost.
  • Accounts created before November 27, 2026 keep the trial they started with, which did not require a card. One trial per business.

2. How to cancel

  • You can cancel at any time from your Acuarius account or by writing to us at support@acuarius.app.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for: until that date you keep using your plan, and after that you are not charged again.
  • There is no penalty and no minimum commitment period.
  • Before closing your account you can export your contacts and your data.

3. Refunds

  • First subscription: if the service does not deliver what we describe, you can request a full refund within the first 7 days of your first subscription, whether monthly or annual.
  • Periods already billed: after those 7 days we do not refund months or years already charged, nor the unused portion of a period.
  • Annual payment ("2 months free"): you pay for 10 months and get 12. If you cancel, the plan stays active until the end of the paid year; the unused portion is not refunded, except within 7 days of the first subscription.
  • SMS credits: they are prepaid and are not refunded in cash. If the provider does not accept a message, its credit is automatically returned to your balance.
  • Charges made in error: if we charge you twice or the wrong amount, we refund the full difference, regardless of the date.
  • Accounts suspended for abuse (spam, fraud or other uses prohibited by the Terms): are not entitled to a refund.

To request a refund, write to us at support@acuarius.app with your account email and the reason. We respond within a maximum of 3 business days. If it is approved, the money goes back to the same payment method; depending on your bank, it may take between 5 and 10 business days to appear.

4. Charges you do not recognize and disputes

  • Charges are billed by Aixion Labs Solutions LLC and appear on your statement as ACUARIUS.
  • If you see a charge you do not recognize, write to us before opening a dispute or a chargeback with your bank: we review it within a maximum of 3 business days and, if there was an error, we refund it.
  • If you open a dispute, we will send your bank the information about your account and your subscription so it can resolve it. While it is open, we may pause the account.

5. Prices and currency

Prices are published and charged in US dollars (USD) and do not include taxes; if the law of your country requires them, they are added to the charge. Your bank may apply its own fee for payments in another currency. If we change the price of your plan, we will notify you at least 30 days in advance.
