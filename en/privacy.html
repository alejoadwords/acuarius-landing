Personal Data Processing Policy

Effective as of November 27, 2026

This English version is a courtesy translation. If it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

This policy explains how we process personal data at Acuarius (app.acuarius.app and acuarius.app), in accordance with Ley 1581 de 2012, Decreto 1377 de 2013 (compiled into Decreto 1074 de 2015) and other Colombian data protection regulations, and with the laws of the other countries where we provide the service.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Johana Liliana Soler Beltran, the individual who operates the Acuarius brand (Colombia)
  • Address: Carrera 87 77-23, 111051 Bogotá D.C., Colombia
  • In Colombia: Check In Col SAS, NIT 900.825.601-2, Carrera 53 #106-280, Centro Empresarial, Barranquilla, provides the SMS service; with respect to the data of that service, it also acts as a controller.
  • Email: support@acuarius.app
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +57 321 997 2333

2. Two types of data, two different roles

Data of our users. The data of people who create an account on Acuarius or are part of an account's team. For this data we are the data controller, and this policy applies in full.

Data of our customers' contacts. The leads, customers and contacts that each company uploads to Acuarius or that reach its account through its forms and channels. For this data the controller is the customer company, and Acuarius acts as the data processor: we process it only on behalf of that company and according to its instructions. If you are one of those contacts and want to exercise your rights, contact the company that wrote to you; if you contact us, we will forward your request to that company.

3. What data we process

  • Account: name, email, company and, if you provide it, phone number. Passwords are managed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Payments: processed by Stripe (and Hotmart, for subscriptions previously purchased through that channel). We receive the buyer's email, the product and the payment status; never your card details.
  • What you upload and create: contacts, inbox conversations, tasks, notes, campaigns, templates, proposals and files.
  • Integrations: the access permissions for the accounts you connect (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Mercado Pago and others). Those for Google, Meta and the messaging channels are stored encrypted; we are finishing encrypting the rest.
  • API: if you create API keys or webhooks, for each key we store only a fingerprint that cannot be used to operate it; each webhook's secret is stored encrypted; and we log what each key does and the notifications we send to your systems.
  • Usage and technical data: activity and error logs, IP address, browser and the cookies described in section 9.

We do not request sensitive data. If a customer's conversation happens to contain it, its processing is the responsibility of the company that the conversation belongs to.

4. What we use it for

  • Creating and managing your account, your team and your permissions
  • Providing the features you use: CRM, inbox, AI agents, automations, campaigns, bookings and reports
  • Charging, invoicing and managing your plan
  • Providing support and notifying you of changes, usage limits or incidents affecting your account
  • Preventing fraud and abuse, for example the mass sending of deceptive messages
  • Measuring and improving the service with aggregated data
  • Sending you communications about Acuarius; you can unsubscribe at any time

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers we need in order to deliver the service, who act as processors, and with the platforms you choose to connect:

  • Infrastructure: Vercel (application) and Supabase (database), in the United States; Clerk (sign-in), in the United States.
  • Artificial intelligence: Anthropic, for the agents and assistants; fal.ai and BytePlus, to generate images and videos when you request them; and OpenAI, Google (Gemini) and Perplexity, only for the report on your brand's visibility in AI search engines, which sends questions about your brand and not data about your contacts.
  • Messaging: Resend, for emails; LabsMobile (001TECH SL, Spain), for SMS; and Meta, for WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.
  • Search: Serper, for the Google ranking lookups in the SEO module.
  • Payments: Stripe (and Hotmart, for earlier subscriptions), for your subscriptions; Mercado Pago, only if you connect it to collect payment for your proposals.
  • Platforms you connect: Google (Ads, Calendar, Drive and Sheets), Meta Ads, TikTok and LinkedIn, which receive what is necessary for the feature you turned on.
  • Authorities: when required by law or a court order.

SMS. If you send text messages, each recipient's number and the content of the message are passed to LabsMobile, which uses them only to deliver them. LabsMobile keeps the sending history accessible for 12 months and for up to 4 more years in backups, and part of its support staff is located in Colombia and Uruguay, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Systems you connect through the API. If you create an API key, connect an agent through MCP or set up a webhook, the data that system queries or that the webhook sends (for example, data about your prospects, their conversations and their tasks) reaches the destination you chose. You order that delivery, as the controller of your contacts' data; whoever receives it is not one of our providers, and what they do with it is their responsibility and yours. We only send what each key's permissions allow and what you chose for each webhook, and only to secure (https) addresses. You can revoke a key or delete a webhook at any time, and sending stops at that moment.

Purchase measurement. When you buy a plan or an add-on, we report that purchase to Meta through its Conversions API to measure our advertising. We send the amount, the currency and your email hashed with SHA-256, a format that Meta can only use to recognize whether it already knows you, not to read it.

Data we receive from Meta (Facebook and Instagram). When you connect your Meta account, your pages or your Instagram account, we receive only what the feature you turn on needs: the performance of your campaigns, the list of your portfolios, pages and accounts so you can choose which ones to connect, the name and photo of those accounts, and the messages people send you through Messenger and Instagram, with the sender's name. We use them only to show them to you in Acuarius, to reply from the inbox and for the actions you order (such as pausing a campaign). We do not sell them, we do not share them with third parties for advertising and we do not use them to train artificial intelligence models. You can revoke access at any time: when you disconnect Meta in Settings → Integrations we delete its token, and when you disconnect a channel in Sources → Configure channels we delete its token and the page stops sending us messages. You can also remove Acuarius from your Facebook account settings. To ask us to delete the data received from Meta, write to us at support@acuarius.app.

6. Google data

If you connect your Google account, Acuarius accesses only what the feature you turned on needs:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): create, change and delete the events for the meetings you schedule in Acuarius, and read your events to show you when you are busy and so that your meeting links and your booking page only offer free times. Only in the calendar you choose (the primary one, if you don't choose another).
  • List of your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly): see the names of your Google calendars so you can choose which one Acuarius works in. This permission does not give access to their events, and we only store which one you chose.
  • Google Ads (adwords): read your campaigns and metrics, create the campaigns you prepare in Acuarius (always paused) and make the changes you request.
  • Google Data Manager (datamanager): send your CRM sales to your Google Ads account as conversions and, if you request it, your customer lists as audiences. Email addresses and phone numbers are sent hashed with SHA-256.

Only you and your team see this data. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, no one on our team reads it unless you ask us to in order to give you support, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models, whether ours or third parties'. You can disconnect Google at any time from Acuarius and also from myaccount.google.com/permissions; when you disconnect it we delete the stored access.

Google user data — Limited Use. Acuarius's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

7. International data transfer

Several of our providers are located outside Colombia, mainly in the United States and Spain, and our data is hosted in the United States (Virginia). By accepting this policy you authorize that international transfer and transmission of data, which we carry out with providers that offer adequate levels of protection and under their data processing agreements.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No one connects directly to the database: only our application accesses it, after verifying your session and your permissions, and your browser never receives its keys. Access credentials for the accounts you connect are stored protected, and encrypted in the case of Google, Meta and the messaging channels. We make daily backups. We run on infrastructure from certified providers; Acuarius as a company does not hold its own security certification. No system is infallible: if an incident affecting your data occurs, we will inform you and report it to the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio when the law requires it.

9. Cookies and analytics

We use necessary cookies to keep your session active and analytics cookies (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) to understand how the service is used. On acuarius.app we also use the Meta pixel to measure our campaigns. You can block analytics and advertising cookies from your browser without losing access to the application.

10. How long we keep it

  • Your account data, for as long as the account exists.
  • What you delete inside the application, such as contacts, goes to a trash bin for 30 days and is then deleted.
  • If you delete your account, we delete its data and the data of its contacts; backups are overwritten in about 7 days.
  • The log of what API keys do and of the notifications sent to your systems, 30 days.
  • Billing and payment records, for as long as commercial and tax regulations require.

11. Your rights

As the owner of your data (data subject), you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your data
  • Request proof of the authorization you gave us
  • Be informed about how we use it
  • Revoke the authorization and request the deletion of your data, when there is no legal or contractual duty to keep it
  • Access your data free of charge
  • File complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio, after having first submitted your request to us

12. How to exercise them

Write to us at support@acuarius.app with your name, your account email and what you are requesting. The Acuarius Support Team is the department responsible for handling these requests.

  • Inquiries: we answer them within a maximum of 10 business days. If we cannot meet that deadline, we will tell you why and answer within the following 5 business days.
  • Claims (correction, update, deletion or non-compliance): we handle them within a maximum of 15 business days. If we cannot meet that deadline, we will tell you why and answer within the following 8 business days. If the claim is incomplete, we will ask you for what is missing within 5 business days of receiving it.

13. Minors

Acuarius is a service for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at people under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, if the change is significant, we will notify you by email or within the platform before it takes effect.
