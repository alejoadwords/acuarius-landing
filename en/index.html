Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Can I use Acuarius just as a CRM, without the marketing side?

Yes, and plenty of people use it that way. The CRM works fully on its own: pipeline, lead records, tasks, calendar, team and reports. The AI agents are there whenever you want them, but you don’t have to touch them to get value from the platform.

Can I bring the contacts I already have?

Yes. Export your list to CSV from Excel, Google Sheets or the CRM you use today, and import it from the pipeline. Acuarius flags repeated emails or phone numbers so you don’t end up with duplicate contacts, and anything you delete stays in the trash for 30 days in case you change your mind.

How do leads from my website get into the CRM?

Three ways. You can build a form in Acuarius and share or embed it; you can connect the form you already have by pasting one line of code on your site, without rebuilding anything; or you can receive the data from any external platform via webhook. In all three cases the lead enters the pipeline with its tag, an assigned owner and a follow-up task.

How many people on my team can work in the CRM?

The license includes 3 users, and you can add more for 9 USD a month each. You invite them by email, each one signs in with their own account and works the leads assigned to them. Leads are distributed in rotation across the team or fixed to one person depending on the source, and you see each rep’s performance in the report.

Do I need a card for the free trial?

Yes. To start the 14 days you add a card through Stripe, but nothing is charged today. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, the monthly Pro plan (39 USD) is charged the next day and renews every month until you cancel. Before that charge you can choose your plan and its add-ons, and you can cancel from your account at any time during the trial, at no cost.

Can I use Acuarius if I’m a freelancer with several clients?

Yes. Each client is a separate profile, with its own pipeline, leads, Google Ads and Meta connections and reports. Your license covers your own business, and each additional client costs 9 USD a month. You switch clients in one click and the agent knows exactly who you’re working with.

Is my campaign data secure?

Yes. All platform access tokens are stored encrypted. We don’t share your data with third parties. You can revoke access at any time from your Acuarius settings or directly in Google/Meta.
