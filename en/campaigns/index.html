What it is

What is a campaign in Acuarius?

A campaign is a message that goes out to a group of contacts in your CRM at a moment you choose. In Acuarius it is sent by email or WhatsApp, to an audience you build from the data you already have: tags, stage, source, your own fields and products.

Bulk WhatsApp messaging from a phone ends in blocked numbers and broadcast lists that only reach people who saved your number. From the CRM it goes out through the official WhatsApp platform, with Meta-approved templates, and every send is logged on the contact's record.

WhatsApp

Why does bulk WhatsApp only work with Meta-approved templates?

Because those are WhatsApp's rules, not Acuarius's. A business can only send a free-form message to someone who wrote to it in the last 24 hours. To message someone first, or after that window, WhatsApp requires a template that Meta has reviewed and approved.

Templates protect your number. Meta measures the quality of what you send: if many people block or report your messages, it can pause the template or limit the number. That is why Acuarius asks Meta for your templates' status every time you build a campaign: one approved yesterday may be paused today.

You do not have to leave Acuarius to get them. On the "Approved WhatsApp template" card, click "Create template", write it with its header, image, button and variables, and submit it for review. More details in WhatsApp templates and broadcasts.

To send, you need your WhatsApp connected to Acuarius. Today it connects through your own business's Meta app; we explain it in CRM with WhatsApp.

Audience

Three ways to choose who gets it

By filters

Tags, pipeline stage, source, a custom field and a product. You see how many contacts match before sending, and you can save the segment as a list.

Saved list

Dynamic lists, resolved at send time with whoever matches the filters at that moment, or static lists, with the exact contacts you saved.

One by one

Pick contact by contact, searching by name or phone. Imported contacts without a process show up too.

By product

Message people who bought a product, or who are "Interested" because it is on one of their deals. Ideal for announcing a restock or a promotion.

By custom field

Segment by your own data: city, budget, event date. We cover it in custom fields.

Do not send to

Exclude lists or tags, however you choose the audience. It applies at send time, not just in the counter.

Products

Announce a product to people who already showed interest

In a WhatsApp campaign, you pick a product from your catalog under "Campaign product". Its name, price and link fill the template's placeholders, and if the template has an image, the product photo goes on top.

Combine it with the product filter in "Interested" mode: you only message people who had that product on a deal. More in products in the CRM and in products.

Sending

How a large campaign goes out

A campaign goes out in batches: the engine checks the queue every 10 minutes and sends as much as it can on each pass. The counter moves as messages go out, so you see how many are done and how many are left.

Campaigns follow the commercial contact hours set by Colombia's Law 2300: Monday to Friday 7:00 to 19:00 and Saturdays 8:00 to 15:00, never on Sundays or holidays. Outside those hours the campaign waits and picks up where it left off, and the list tells you when it will continue. You can also check "Schedule sending" and pick a date and time.

On WhatsApp, Acuarius respects the daily message limit Meta gives your number. Whatever does not fit today goes out the next day: pushing past that ceiling is how numbers get lost.

Each person gets the campaign only once, even if they are in two processes. And if they unsubscribed from email through one deal, they do not get it through any. An email that bounced or was marked as spam is removed from all following campaigns, and the contact's record says so.

Getting started

Send your first campaign

Choose the channel

Go to Marketing → Campaigns, click "New campaign" and choose Email or WhatsApp.

Write the message

For email: "Campaign name", "Subject", "Preheader", "Sender" and "Reply to", then the content. For WhatsApp: choose the approved template. You can start from a template or use "Write with AI".

Choose the audience

Under "Who gets it?" use "By filters", "Saved list" or "One by one", and add whatever should not receive it under "Do not send to". For email, send a test email first.

Review and send

Under "Review and send", check the preview and the recipients, then click "Send campaign" or "Schedule send".

Common mistakes

Common mistakes in bulk messaging

Sending the same message to your whole database. A campaign to people interested in a product performs better than one to everyone. Use tags, fields or products.

Using a marketing template for everything. If many people block your messages, Meta lowers the template's quality rating and may pause it. Send less and target better.

Not checking failures. Every contact who did not get it has the reason on their record, in the Campaigns box: no phone, unsubscribed, invalid email format or what WhatsApp replied.

Ignoring people who ask to stop in other words. Unsubscribing is automatic for email and, on WhatsApp, when they reply PARAR (STOP). If someone asks another way ("please stop messaging me"), tag them and exclude that tag under "Do not send to".

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

How do I send bulk WhatsApp messages?

With your WhatsApp connected to Acuarius, create a WhatsApp campaign, choose a Meta-approved template, build the audience and send it. The template lets you message contacts who have not written to you in the last 24 hours.

Why do I need an approved template?

WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window, any business needs a Meta-approved template. You can create it and submit it for review from Acuarius.

Can I send bulk emails with my own design?

Yes. You can use a header image, button and color, or a design from your email templates. It is sent from the Acuarius domain under your business name, and replies go to the address you set in "Reply to".

Do campaigns go out instantly?

They go out in batches, with the engine checking the queue every 10 minutes, and only within the hours set by Law 2300. A large campaign can take a while; the counter moves as it sends.

What happens with people who unsubscribe?

For email, every message has its unsubscribe link. Whoever clicks it gets no more email campaigns, even if they are in several processes. On WhatsApp, whoever replies PARAR (STOP) or taps "Stop notifying me" gets no more WhatsApp campaigns or automation steps: they show up as skipped, with the reason.

How do I know who did not get it?

The campaign shows sent, skipped and failed. On each contact's record, the Campaigns box shows what they received and, if it did not reach them, the reason.

Can I schedule a campaign?

Yes. In the last step, check "Schedule sending" and pick the date and time.

How is it different from an automation?

A campaign messages a group at one moment. An automation messages each lead when something happens to them: they come in, change stage, book or buy. To keep the conversation going after the send, your AI agent answers whoever replies.
